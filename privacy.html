1. Introduction

Welcome to ResumeCraft (“we,” “our,” or “us”). This Privacy Policy explains how SkyLine Tech Ventures (“Company,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you use the ResumeCraft mobile application (“App”) available on Google Play.

By downloading, installing, or using ResumeCraft, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the App.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you use ResumeCraft:

  • Resume Content: Information you enter into the App to create your resume, including name, contact details, work experience, education, skills, and other professional information.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the App, including features used, session duration, and crash reports.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID and similar identifiers used for advertising purposes (where applicable).
  • Log Data: IP address, browser type (for web components), access times, and pages viewed within the App.

Resume data you create is stored locally on your device unless you explicitly choose to sync or back up data through features we may offer in the future. We do not require account registration to use core App features.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the ResumeCraft App and its features
  • Enable resume creation, editing, and PDF export functionality
  • Deliver AI-powered resume assistance and template recommendations
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve user experience and App performance
  • Display personalized advertisements through third-party ad networks
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues and security vulnerabilities
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

4. AdMob Advertising

ResumeCraft uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data about you to serve personalized ads, including your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, and usage information.

You can opt out of personalized advertising on your Android device by going to Settings → Google → Ads and enabling “Opt out of Ads Personalization.”

For more information about how Google uses data, visit: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob & Privacy.

5. Analytics Services

We may use analytics services such as Google Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with ResumeCraft. These services may collect information such as device identifiers, session data, and feature usage statistics.

Analytics data is used in aggregated form to improve App performance, fix bugs, and enhance user experience. We do not sell your personal resume content to analytics providers.

ResumeCraft may integrate with or link to third-party services, including:

These third parties have their own privacy policies governing how they collect and use your information. We encourage you to review their policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.

7. Data Security

We implement commercially reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Resume data stored locally on your device is your responsibility to protect. We recommend using device-level security features such as screen locks and encrypted storage where available.

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email address provided below. We will respond to your request within a reasonable timeframe as required by applicable law.
